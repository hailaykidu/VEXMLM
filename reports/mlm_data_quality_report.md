**Scale caveat.** After deduplication these are roughly **1.2M and 1.1M tokens**
respectively. That is small for continued pretraining: at max_seq_length 256 the
Amharic corpus is on the order of 5K–7K full sequences, so a single epoch is a
few hundred optimizer steps at batch 32. Ten epochs over data this size risks
memorization rather than generalization, and makes the held-out perplexity
optimistic. Worth revisiting before Stage 1 — see the training plan.

## Duplication (the actionable finding)

| Metric | Amharic | Tigrinya |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate lines | 70,137 | 58,985 |
| Duplicate rate | **35.07%** | **29.49%** |
| Distinct lines repeated ≥2× | 32,932 | 37,677 |
| Highest repeat count | 131× | 160× |

The repeats are concentrated, not uniform — a signature of crawl artifacts
rather than natural recurrence. The most frequent lines are religious verses:

| Corpus | Repeats | Line (truncated) |
|---|---|---|
| Amharic | 131× | `ሁለቱንም በምር እንጅ አልፈጠርናቸውም፤ ግን አብዛኞቻቸው አያውቁም።` |
| Amharic | 105× | `"እስከሚቀሰቀሱበት ቀን ድረስ አቆየኝ" አለ።` |
| Tigrinya | 160× | `ከምቲ ንነቢይ ሙሴ ዝተዛረቦ እግዚኣብሔር "ስመይ ኣብኡ ተሰምዩ እዩ እሞ…` |
| Tigrinya | 148× | `ካብ'ቲ ዝተወለድኩዎ ዝዓበኹዎን ዝኸፍአ ክፉእ የሎን።` |

**Why this matters.** Duplicated sentences are effectively upsampled: a line
repeated 160× contributes 160 gradient updates per epoch. That biases the model
toward the duplicated register (here, scripture), inflates apparent training
progress, and — critically — leaks between train and validation, since an
identical line can land on both sides of a random split. Held-out perplexity
would then be measured partly on text the model has already seen verbatim.

**Handled.** `datasets/prepare.py` deduplicates exactly before splitting, so the
pipeline as configured never trains on the raw files. Post-deduplication counts
are recorded in `datasets/processed/preparation_report.json`. Run it before
Stage 1:

```bash
python datasets/prepare.py --config configs/base.yaml
```

Coverage is strong: 318 and 345 distinct Ethiopic codepoints exercise the script

## Encoding

| Issue | Amharic | Tigrinya |
|---|---|---|
| U+FFFD replacement characters | 1 | 8 |
| Lines with zero-width/bidi controls | 0 | 0 |
| Lines not in NFC form | 0 | 0 |
| Lines containing a URL | 7 | 29 |

Nine replacement characters across 16M characters (~0.00006%) indicate a small
number of bytes lost upstream — negligible, and the affected lines are dropped
or truncated by normal filtering. The corpora are **already NFC-normalized**, so
`prepare.py`'s NFC pass is a no-op here; it remains necessary for other inputs.

No byte-level-BPE mojibake was detected. The encoding defect documented in
[`docs/REFERENCE_ARTIFACTS.md`](../docs/REFERENCE_ARTIFACTS.md) does **not**
affect these corpora.

## Length distribution (characters per line)

| Percentile | Amharic | Tigrinya |
|---|---|---|
| min | 8 | 9 |
| p10 | 20 | 15 |
| p25 | 27 | 21 |
| median | 40 | 30 |
| p75 | 58 | 43 |
| p90 | 78 | 60 |
| p99 | 120 | 109 |
| max | 329 | 340 |

Lines are short — median 40 and 30 characters, roughly 8 and 6 tokens. At
`max_seq_length=256` almost every line is padded rather than truncated, wasting
most of each sequence.

**Recommendation:** concatenate and chunk lines into full-length blocks for
Stage 1, instead of treating one line as one example. Standard practice for MLM,
and here it would cut wasted compute by roughly an order of magnitude. This is a
training-efficiency change, not a method change, and is **not** applied by
default — the implementation is frozen (see
[`docs/IMPLEMENTATION_FREEZE.md`](../docs/IMPLEMENTATION_FREEZE.md)). Raised for
a decision before Stage 1 launches.
